# Third-Party Notices and Research Sources

**Maintainer:** Amirali Yazdani

Multi-Pascal Mac is a clean-room reimplementation of a teaching-language environment. The source tree does not include the original MPWin executable or its manual.

The language-recovery notes in [`docs/MPWinSpec.md`](docs/MPWinSpec.md) cite the historical MPWin documentation and exercise sources used to understand its syntax and behavior. They include a limited set of diagnostic strings and language facts attributed there to the original software. Those historical references should not be mistaken for authorship of the original MPWin language or application.

The compiler-research document [`docs/COMPILER_CHOICES.md`](docs/COMPILER_CHOICES.md) links to external open-source projects for background research; none of those projects is bundled as a dependency in this repository. Their own licenses apply to their respective codebases.
